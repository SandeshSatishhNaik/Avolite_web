# Redesign direction derivation

AVOLITE's unique mechanism is a proposed feedback-driven scan decision loop, supported today by a separate simulated DSP foundation. Judges encounter it during rapid evaluation and may later inspect the technical evidence. Its cultural home is an engineering demonstration and design review.

Avoid the default glowing radar HUD and its predictable cream editorial opposite. Directions are proposals, not approved changes.

Seven candidates, ordered by audience resonance:
1. **Signal Atlas** — scientific atlas plates: a large explanatory system drawing paired with a compact evidence index. Deep marine fields and pale technical plates; readable without decorative telemetry.
2. **Review Wall** — architecture review boards: large cobalt chapter planes, precise white drawings, marginal evidence annotations, and an explicit implemented/designed boundary.
3. **Instrument Bench** — laboratory instrument fascia: charcoal metal, subdued amber controls, unambiguous state strips, and plot windows with literal units.
4. **Transit Logic** — transport wayfinding: indigo routing, clear chapter destinations, interchange-like decision points, and continuous reading paths.
5. **Engineering Folio** — bound technical folios: graphite surfaces, restrained celadon index tabs, foldout diagrams, and a deliberate evidence appendix.
6. **Exhibition Cutaway** — science museum exhibits: midnight plum fields, oversized pale cutaway drawings, clear captions, and a visitor-controlled reveal.
7. **Protocol Ledger** — standards and verification registers: slate surfaces, electric lime used sparingly for selection, numbered clauses, and side-by-side claims and evidence.

Families: scientific publication, review/exhibition environments, physical instruments, public wayfinding, and standards registers. The visual story must retain honest status boundaries in every direction.
